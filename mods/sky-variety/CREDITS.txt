Downloaded 2026-11-08 as 8K tonemapped JPGs. Each upper hemisphere was
cropped and resized to the target sheet's aspect; wrapped edges matched.
Sky sheets use new alpha with an 8% zenith fade; gradient RGB comes from
the source panorama's horizontal colour average. The runtime retains only
the gradient atlas alpha. Night art is darkened to 55% for a night sky.
No original game textures or masks are bundled.
