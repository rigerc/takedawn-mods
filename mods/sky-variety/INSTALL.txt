Sky Variety 1.2.1

One mod, eleven skies assigned across 37 track variants. See coverage.tsv for
exact assignments. The game chooses by the track's original texture hash;
there is no separate selector or random change on each race.

Extract sky-variety/ into the default mods folder:
  Linux / Steam Deck: ~/.local/share/takedawn/mods/
  macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Takedawn/mods/
  Windows: %APPDATA%\Takedawn\mods\
  Portable: data/mods/ next to the executable
Enable Sky Variety in Settings > Mods and restart.
Disable Industrial Sunset Skies or any other sky pack first: these replace
the same texture hashes. For the original skies, disable this mod and restart.

17 RGBA sky sheets at 4x original size (4096x1024 or 4096x512), plus
20 matching RGB gradient atlases (128x128). alpha=auto reads new sheet alpha
and retains the gradient atlas alpha. Sheet opacity is full except an 8%
zenith fade. The old cloud silhouettes are not reused.
Requires Takedawn's PNG texture-pack support (docs/mod-folders.md).
Sky gradients are recoloured from each source panorama. Fog, sun/moon sprites,
track lighting and reflection textures remain in use. This replaces sky artwork; it does not change time of day.
Night artwork on a track does not darken the road. Colours can be affected by
the game's remaining vertex tint and fog. Track variants sharing a hash share a sky.
All assignments identified from NTSC-U disc data. All 37 variants have not
been visually verified. See VALIDATION.txt for checks actually performed.
